Who We Are

An esports org built around opportunity

SkyReign Global is an esports organization focused on competitive opportunity, player visibility, community and storytelling.

The organization believes that talented players should not remain invisible simply because they lack access to established networks, professional stages or large audiences.

SkyReign Global is building scalable event properties that combine online participation, offline competition, creator culture and live experiences.

Vision and mission

Our Mission

Turning overlooked players into recognized performers

To create credible competitive stages that transform overlooked players into recognized performers.

Our Vision

A recognizable esports ecosystem

To build a recognizable esports ecosystem where emerging players are discovered, communities grow around their journeys and brands participate in meaningful gaming experiences.
